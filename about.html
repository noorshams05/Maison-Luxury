Our Maison

A Thoughtful Gift.
A Beautiful Beginning.

Luxury follows you.

Maison by MS Luxury Homes began with a simple question: What do you give someone who seemingly has everything?

For founder Michele Sullivan, the answer grew out of more than 20 years in luxury real estate. Working with an ultra-high-net-worth clientele, she wanted her closing gifts to feel as personal and considered as the homes she helped her clients find.

“A bottle of Dom Pérignon is a lovely gift, but I imagined it joining countless others in a client’s cellar,” Michele recalls. “I wanted to give something memorable. Something luxurious that they might not already own, chosen especially for them.”

She began gifting bespoke robes embroidered with her clients’ initials. The response revealed an opportunity to bring that same personal touch to a broader collection.

In 2026, Michele launched the MS Luxury Home Collection with a belief that continues to guide Maison: everyone deserves to experience luxury in their own home, regardless of its price.

What began as a gesture of gratitude became a collection devoted to making everyday rituals feel special. Each piece reflects Michele’s love of beautiful homes and the thoughtful details that make them deeply personal.

Luxury follows you.

From my house, Maison by MS Luxury, to your home.

With love,
Michele Sullivan
Founder
